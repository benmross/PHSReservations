Guide Page

How do I reserve a location?

On the calendar page, you can sort by time, date, or hub. From there, you can select at least one option and provide your name and reason for booking. Click "enter" to send that off and reserve the room.

Can I reserve the same location every week?

To do this, sort the calendar by time and location. It will then show a list of dates that you can reserve together.

Can I keep my purpose confidential?

Absolutely! There will be a button labeled "private?" that, when selected, will hide the usage of the room from other users, save Ms. Wilder. It will still show up as booked on the map viewer.

Still have questions?
